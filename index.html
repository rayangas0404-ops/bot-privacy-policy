Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord community bot processes information. The bot helps users create, organize, and manage their Discord servers.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and messages containing commands. It may also process information required for tickets, moderation, and server management.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to execute commands, manage server settings, operate ticket systems, provide moderation features, and maintain the bot.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be stored when required for the bot's features. Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Deletion

Users may contact the bot owner through the official support server to ask questions or request deletion of stored data.

7. Third-Party Services

The bot uses Discord and a hosting provider. Their respective terms and privacy policies may also apply.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot owner through the official Discord support server.
